# Roadmap: V0 to V3

Each rung ships and demos on its own. The ordering is deliberate and inverting
it is how these projects die: **verifiable client, then secrecy, then
anonymity, then coercion mitigation.**

The reason for that order: client verifiability is the thing we uniquely have
(VISION.md section 3), and every later rung's cryptography is worthless
without it -- encrypting a ballot the client already misread proves nothing.

Facts below were verified in July 2026; sources at the end. Re-check the
load-bearing numbers before depending on them, per ic-git ROADMAP.md
discipline.

## Verified IC primitives

**vetKD / vetKeys -- PRODUCTION, since June 2025.** Better than assumed; no
need to design around a test key.

- Production key `Bls12_381_G2:key_1` generated by NNS proposal **137075** on
  the fiduciary subnet `pzp6e` (2025-06-20), backed up to the Internet
  Identity subnet `uzr34` via proposals 137123 / 137130 / 137131 (2025-06-26,
  ~15 min planned downtime), enabled for public canister use 2025-06-27.
- Management canister API: `vetkd_public_key`, `vetkd_derive_key`.
- Libraries: `dfinity/vetkeys` (frontend + backend).
- **Footgun:** examples use `insecure_test_key_1`. Shipping that to mainnet is
  a silent, total loss of ballot secrecy -- no error, no warning, just no
  security. Same class of mistake as the `CARGO_NET_LOCKED` no-op ic-git
  caught in REPRODUCIBLE_BUILD.md. Add a deploy-time assertion that the key id
  is `key_1` on mainnet.
- **Unverified:** per-call cycle cost. Check the cycle-costs reference before
  budgeting an election-sized number of derivations.

**Threshold ECDSA / Ed25519** -- GA; already used by ic-git for cross-chain
checkpointing. Relevant at V0 for anchoring the final tally hash.

**Certified state / response certification** -- the basis of the bundle
verification; ic-git's `docs/ATTESTATION.md` specifies the certified
module-hash read (BLS12-381 + pinned NNS root key + freshness). **That reader
is the highest-risk unbuilt component in the whole stack and ic-vote depends
on it.** It is the first thing to prototype, in ic-git, not here.

## V0 -- verifiable client, public ballots

**No secrecy at all.** Deliberately.

- A poll canister holding: an eligibility roll, an open ballot box, and a
  tally anyone can recompute from public data.
- Ballots signed by roll-listed identities. Double-voting prevented by
  identity. Everything public.
- The frontend lives in this repo, is pushed to ic-git, served by the git
  canister from the committed bundle, and attested in the ProvenanceRegistry
  as `<repo>#site`.
- The voter-facing verifier reports GREEN/YELLOW/RED per THREAT_MODEL.md
  section 4 before the ballot can be marked.
- Final tally hash checkpointed to the registry, and to Solana once ic-git's
  S1 lands -- two independent trust roots.

**What V0 proves:** the page that took your vote was the reviewed page. That
is the novel claim, and it is provable with zero novel cryptography. Nothing
here needs vetKD, ZK, or a mixnet.

**What V0 is suitable for:** votes that are *already* public -- many board
votes, most shareholder votes, recorded-vote union motions, DAO governance.
That is a real deployable use case, not a toy.

**Dogfooding:** the first real election run on V0 should be a decision about
ic-git itself.

## V1 -- ballot secrecy

- Ballots encrypted client-side under a vetKD-derived key; the canister never
  holds plaintext or a decryption key.
- Threshold decrypt at close, or homomorphic tally without ever decrypting
  individual ballots (preferred where the ballot type allows it -- it removes
  an entire class of "who decrypted early" concern).
- **Re-voting support lands here**, not later: a voter may recast during the
  window, last ballot counts. It interacts with the tally and nullifier design
  and cannot be bolted on afterward. This is the only coercion mitigation with
  a track record (Estonia).
- Bulletin board becomes the public record of encrypted ballots plus proofs.

Open design question: homomorphic vs. mixnet. Homomorphic is simpler and
sufficient for yes/no and single-choice-from-small-slate; mixnets are needed
for ranked or write-in ballots. Start homomorphic and be explicit that ballot
types are restricted.

## V2 -- anonymous but eligible

- Roll published as a Merkle root.
- Voter proves in zero knowledge: "I know a leaf in this root, and here is a
  nullifier derived from my key and this election id." Semaphore-shaped.
- Nullifier makes a second ballot from the same member detectable without
  linking either ballot to that member.
- Groth16 verification in a canister is cheap; proving runs client-side in
  wasm. **The prover is a bundle too** -- it goes in the same attested
  frontend, or the anonymity guarantee has an unverified component.

**Eligibility issuance ladder** (T4 in the threat model; pick per deployment,
do not pretend one is universal):

1. **Closed roll** -- organization supplies the member list. V0/V2 default.
   Honest, sufficient for the target market, no new trust root.
2. **Government eID** -- EUDI wallet credentials, BBS+ selective disclosure.
   Real, deployed, jurisdiction-bound.
3. **Passport NFC** -- ZK proof over the ICAO 9303 passive-authentication
   signature (zkPassport / Anon Aadhaar / OpenPassport class). Proves "holds a
   valid passport from country X" without revealing identity. The most
   interesting option and the least mature.

Internet Identity is **not** on this ladder: it gives per-origin pseudonyms,
which is unlinkability, not uniqueness.

## V3 -- coercion mitigation, such as it is

Realistically: re-voting (already in V1), plus UI affordances that make silent
recasting easy and unobservable, plus voter education. We are not shipping
JCJ/Civitas fake credentials -- see THREAT_MODEL.md section 3.

If V3 turns out to be "V1's re-voting plus honest documentation," that is an
acceptable and truthful outcome.

## Dependencies on ic-git

ic-vote cannot ship V0 until ic-git provides:

1. **Certified module-hash reader** (BLS + NNS root key + freshness) --
   `docs/ATTESTATION.md`, unbuilt, highest risk.
2. **Registry `attest()` + K-of-N verifier tooling** -- specified, unbuilt.
   The live Sepolia registry is the `set`/`get` one.
3. **`registry_publish_site` exercised on mainnet** -- shipped, reported
   untested against mainnet.
4. **Multi-chain EVM config** -- currently single-chain (Sepolia); needed to
   put the election's provenance on the chain the organization actually uses.

Items 1 and 2 are the ic-git critical path for this project. Nothing in
ic-vote should start before item 1 has a prototype, because a failure there
changes the whole design.
